# Acme Logistics: Company Overview

## Who we are

Acme Logistics is a freight and supply-chain company founded in **2009** and headquartered in **Memphis, Tennessee**. We employ approximately **4,200 people** and operate **38 distribution centers across 21 states**, supported by a fleet of **1,850 trucks**.

## Leadership

Our Chief Executive Officer is Rebecca Lang, who joined in 2016 and became CEO in 2021. Our Chief Financial Officer is Marcus Oyelaran.

## Services

- **Standard freight:** full-truckload and less-than-truckload shipping across the continental United States.
- **Cold chain:** temperature-controlled transport for food and pharmaceutical customers, monitored in real time.
- **Last mile:** residential and business delivery in 140 metropolitan areas, with two-hour delivery windows.

## Performance

In 2025, Acme delivered **96.4% of shipments on time**, up from 94.1% in 2024. Our customer satisfaction score for 2025 was 4.6 out of 5.

## Sustainability

Acme has committed to reducing fleet greenhouse gas emissions by **30% by 2030**, measured against a 2022 baseline. We are meeting this goal through route optimization, driver training, and a phased move to electric trucks in urban delivery.

## Our values

1. Safety first: no delivery is worth an injury.
2. Keep the promise: on time, intact, every time.
3. Own the outcome: we fix problems instead of assigning blame.
4. Grow together: we invest in our people and our communities.
