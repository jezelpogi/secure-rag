# Acme Logistics: Freight Claims and Returns Policy

Version 3.2, effective January 1, 2026. Applies to all standard customer shipments.

## Filing a damage or loss claim

Customers must report damaged freight within **7 calendar days** of delivery. Lost shipments may be reported once a shipment is 10 days past its estimated delivery date. Claims can be filed on the customer portal at claims.acmelogistics.com or by phone at 1-800-555-0134 (Monday to Friday, 7:00 AM to 7:00 PM Central Time).

## Required documentation

Every claim must include the bill of lading number and at least three photos of the damage, taken before the packaging is discarded. Claims over **$10,000** also require a carrier inspection report. Acme schedules these inspections within 3 business days of the claim being filed.

## Liability limits

Standard liability is **$0.50 per pound** per package. Customers who need more protection can buy declared value coverage, which costs **1.2% of the declared value**. Declared value coverage is available up to **$250,000 per shipment**.

## Refunds and returns

Approved claims are paid within **12 business days** to the original payment method. Customers may return undamaged goods within 30 days of delivery. A **15% restocking fee** applies to these returns and is waived when the goods arrived damaged.

## Refused or undeliverable shipments

If a recipient refuses delivery, Acme holds the shipment at the nearest distribution center for 5 days at no charge. After that, storage is billed at $45 per day. Shipments left unclaimed for 60 days are returned to the sender at the sender's cost.

## Questions

Contact Customer Care at 1-800-555-0134 or through the portal.
