# Acme Logistics: Q3 2026 Operating Budget (Finance Internal)

Prepared by the Finance department. Confidential: Finance and executive staff only.

## Summary

The total operating budget for Q3 2026 is **$48.6 million**. This excludes capital expenditure, which is budgeted separately.

## Capital expenditure

Q3 capital expenditure is **$7.5 million**, allocated to the purchase of **60 electric trucks** for urban last-mile routes. Delivery is expected in October 2026.

## Hiring

A hiring freeze is in effect through **September 30, 2026**. Exceptions apply only to **drivers and safety roles**. Any other exception requires CFO approval.

## Fuel assumptions

The fuel budget assumes an average diesel price of $3.85 per gallon. Every $0.10 increase adds about $370,000 to the quarter's fuel cost. The Finance team reviews fuel prices every two weeks.
