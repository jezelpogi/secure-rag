# Acme Logistics: Travel and Expense Policy (Finance Internal)

Owner: Finance. Version 5.0, effective February 1, 2026. Internal: Finance staff only.

## Approval thresholds

- Expenses up to $2,500 need approval from the employee's direct manager.
- Expenses **over $2,500** need approval from a **Vice President**.
- Expenses **over $25,000** need approval from the **Chief Financial Officer**.

## Meals and lodging

- Meal per diem is **$65 per day** for domestic travel and **$85 per day** for international travel.
- Hotel rates are capped at **$210 per night**, or **$320 per night** in New York City and San Francisco.

## Air and ground travel

Employees fly economy class for flights under **6 hours**. Flights of 6 hours or more may be booked in premium economy. Personal vehicle use is reimbursed at **$0.67 per mile**.

## Corporate cards

Corporate cards have a default limit of **$5,000**. Limit increases must be approved by a Vice President and are valid for 90 days.

## Receipts and reimbursement

A receipt is required for any expense over **$25**. Expense reports must be submitted within **30 days** of the expense. Approved reports are reimbursed by direct deposit within **10 business days**.
