# Acme Logistics: Employee Leave Policy (HR Internal)

Owner: Human Resources. Version 4.1, effective January 1, 2026. Confidential: HR staff only.

## Parental leave

Acme provides **16 weeks of paid parental leave** to the primary caregiver and **6 weeks** to the secondary caregiver. Employees become eligible after **9 months of continuous employment**. Leave may be taken in one block or split into two blocks within 12 months of the birth or adoption.

## Vacation

Vacation accrues by years of service:

| Years of service | Vacation days per year |
|------------------|------------------------|
| 1 to 3           | 15                     |
| 4 to 7           | 20                     |
| 8 or more        | 25                     |

Employees may carry over a maximum of **5 unused days** into the next calendar year. Days above the cap are forfeited on December 31.

## Sick leave and bereavement

Employees receive **8 paid sick days** per year. Sick days do not carry over. Bereavement leave is **5 days** for immediate family (spouse, child, parent, sibling) and **3 days** for extended family.

## Requesting leave

Planned leave must be requested at least **30 days in advance** through the HR portal. The employee's manager has 5 business days to approve or decline. Unplanned leave should be reported to the manager and HR by the start of the employee's shift.
