# Acme Logistics: Performance Review Process (HR Internal)

Owner: Human Resources. Version 2.7. Confidential: HR staff only.

## Review cycle

Formal reviews happen twice a year, in **May** and **November**. Managers submit written reviews and ratings in the HR portal, then meet with each employee to discuss them. Department heads hold calibration meetings two weeks before ratings are finalized.

## Rating scale

Employees are rated from 1 to 5:

- **5: Exceptional.** Capped at **10% of any team** to keep the rating meaningful.
- **4: Exceeds expectations.**
- **3: Meets expectations.**
- **2: Needs improvement.**
- **1: Unsatisfactory.**

## Merit increases

Merit raises are tied to the November rating and take effect January 1:

| Rating | Merit increase |
|--------|----------------|
| 5      | 6%             |
| 4      | 4%             |
| 3      | 2.5%           |
| 2 or 1 | 0%             |

## Promotions

A promotion requires **two consecutive ratings of 4 or higher** and a written recommendation from the department head.

## Performance improvement plans

An employee rated 2 or below may be placed on a **60-day performance improvement plan (PIP)**. The plan lists measurable goals and includes check-ins every two weeks. If the goals are not met, HR reviews the case for further action.

## Appeals

An employee may appeal a rating within **10 business days** of receiving it. Appeals go to the **HR Director**, who issues a decision within 15 business days.
